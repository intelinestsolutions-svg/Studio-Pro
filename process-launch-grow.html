Why this stage exists

A great site that launches badly is a bad site.

Most launch problems are avoidable: broken links, lost rankings, missing content, nobody trained. This stage removes them one by one — then keeps the site improving.

01

Findable from day one

Titles, structure, sitemap and Google Business Profile are finished before launch — not “something we'll do later”, when the rankings are already gone.

02

Tested where customers are

Real phones, real browsers, real forms. Every page, link and button is clicked through before you announce anything.

03

A calm launch

Backup first, then deploy, then redirects, then a live smoke test — with monitoring on. And your old site stays reachable until the new one is proven.

04

Nobody left guessing

Recorded training, a written guide, all your logins, and 30 days of fix-cover — so the handover does not end with “good luck”.

Rule 01Nothing goes live without sign-off

The launch date is only confirmed after your written approval of the staging site.

Rule 02Back up before every change

A full backup is taken before anything is deployed, so rollback is always one step away.

Rule 03Old rankings are protected

Every old URL gets a 301 redirect, so Google passes your existing authority to the new pages.

Rule 0430-day fix warranty

If something we built misbehaves in the first 30 days, we fix it at no cost.

Step by step

Six steps, in plain order.

Five steps take your site live in about ten days. The sixth is the part that keeps it earning.

01

Final content & on-page SEO

Day 1–4

Before anything is tested or launched, the content has to be real. We load it, optimise it and make sure every page is legible to Google.

What we do

  • Load final copy, images, prices and contact details
  • Write titles, meta descriptions and heading structure
  • Compress and convert images (WebP), add alt text
  • Add structured data, internal links, sitemap and robots

What you receive

  • Page-by-page SEO checklist you keep
  • Google Search Console & Analytics access
  • Google Business Profile aligned with the new site

What we need from you

  • Final approval of all wording
  • Business details: name, address, phone, service areas
  • Real photos, testimonials and any certifications
02

Testing & quality assurance

Day 3–6

Every page, form, link and menu gets clicked on real devices and real browsers. We would rather find the problem now than in front of your customers.

What we do

  • Device testing: iOS, Android, tablet, laptop
  • Browser testing: Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge
  • Forms, notifications, maps, links and menus checked
  • Speed (Core Web Vitals), accessibility and security pass

What you receive

  • QA report with a clear fix list
  • Speed results before and after fixes
  • Analytics verification so numbers are trustworthy

What we need from you

  • Your own walkthrough on the device you actually use
  • One list of issues within 48 hours
03

Client review & approval

Day 6–7 Approval gate

The last stop before the public sees anything. You review the finished site on staging and tell us, in writing, that it is ready to go live.

What we do

  • Give you the complete staging site and a review checklist
  • Fix everything on your list
  • Agree the launch date and time with you
  • Prepare backups, redirect map and the launch runbook

What you receive

  • Final preview of the exact site that will go live
  • Confirmed launch date and who will be available
  • Redirect map for your old URLs

What we need from you

  • Final written approval
  • Nominate one person to confirm the launch window
04

Launch day

Day 7–8

Short, careful and reversible. Backup first, deploy second, verify third — then we watch it while you tell the world.

What we do

  • Full backup of the current site and data
  • Deploy to the live server, confirm SSL and security
  • Switch DNS, apply every 301 redirect
  • Live smoke test: pages, forms, analytics, uptime monitoring

What you receive

  • Your live website
  • Launch report: what changed, what was checked
  • Confirmation that Search Console has the new sitemap

What we need from you

  • Domain / hosting access (or let us handle both)
  • Someone reachable for a two-hour window
05

Training & handover

Day 8–10

Your team leaves this stage able to run the site: how to edit a page, add a photo, publish a post and read the numbers.

What we do

  • Run a 45-minute training session and record it
  • Hand over every login and a written editing guide
  • Set up backups, updates and uptime monitoring
  • Start the 30-day warranty window

What you receive

  • Handover pack: logins, docs, recording, checklist
  • Content calendar for the first months (optional)
  • 30 days of free fixes for anything we got wrong

What we need from you

  • Nominate the person (or people) who will edit the site
  • Save the handover pack somewhere your team can find it
06

Grow — ongoing SEO & care

Monthly · after launch

Launch is a start line, not a finish line. This optional monthly work is what turns a website into a steady source of customers.

What we do

  • Track your keywords and local rankings, month by month
  • Publish optimised content (blog, service pages, offers)
  • Post on Google Business Profile and build local signals
  • Improve conversions: headlines, calls-to-action, page order
  • Keep everything patched, backed up and fast

What you receive

  • Monthly report: rankings, traffic, enquiries — in plain words
  • A work log showing exactly what we did
  • Priority support for your site

What we need from you

  • Approve topics and share news, offers and projects
  • Tell us what those enquiries actually turned into

The 30-day warranty

What it covers — and what it does not.

For 30 days after launch, anything we built that misbehaves is fixed free of charge. It is our way of saying we test hard and stand behind the work.

  • Covered: bugs, broken forms, layout issues, speed regressions, anything we configured wrongly.
  • Covered: small content updates we agreed to make at launch.
  • Not covered: brand-new features — those are quoted, as always, before work starts.
  • Not covered: problems caused by third-party plugins or tools you install yourself — but we will always tell you what went wrong.
Questions

Before you go live.

What clients ask us most about launching — and growing — a website.

Are hosting and domain name included in the price?
Yes — a website including hosting and domain name starts at as low as RM1,600.00, so you are not billed separately for them on launch day. Every login is handed to you at handover, or we keep managing hosting for you as part of the monthly plan.
How long until the site is actually live?
About 10 working days after Stage 02 finishes, provided content and approvals arrive on time. The biggest variable is usually content, not code — so we start chasing it while the site is still being built.
Will I lose my Google rankings?
You should not. Every old URL is mapped to a new one with a 301 redirect, titles and structure stay consistent, and we submit the new sitemap to Google Search Console the same day. Some movement in the first 1–2 weeks is normal while Google re-crawls; we monitor it with you.
Do I have to be there on launch day?
You need one person reachable for a two-hour window to confirm everything looks right — that is all. Most launches take under an hour, and we handle backups, DNS, SSL and testing. If anything unexpected happens, we roll back to the backup immediately.
Do I get training?
Yes: a 45-minute session (recorded so new staff can watch it later), a written editing guide, and every login. You leave able to edit pages, add images, publish posts and read your traffic report without calling anyone.
What exactly does the 30-day warranty cover?
Anything we built that misbehaves — bugs, broken forms, layout problems, configuration mistakes — is fixed free within 30 days of launch. New features and third-party issues are outside it, and we always explain the difference plainly.
Do you guarantee first-page rankings?
No — and you should be suspicious of anyone who does. No agency controls Google. What we do guarantee is correct technical SEO, honest monthly reporting, and steady work on the things that actually move rankings: content, local signals, authority and a fast site.
What does the monthly growth plan include?
Keyword and local rank tracking, new or refreshed content (blog, service pages, offers), Google Business Profile activity, conversion improvements to your key pages, plus hosting care — backups, updates, security and speed checks. Every month you get a plain-language report and a work log.
Can we stop the monthly plan?
Yes. It is monthly, not a long lock-in — cancel with notice and the site, content and logins stay entirely yours. We will also tell you honestly if we think you are paying for work that is not helping.
What if something breaks after the warranty period?
Message us. Small fixes are often nothing; if it needs real work we quote it before starting. Clients on the monthly plan get priority support as part of it.
Can you take over a site someone else built?
Yes. We audit it, migrate content, clean up technical problems and set up tracking — then run the same launch checklist. If the existing site is sound, we will tell you that too rather than sell you a rebuild you do not need.
