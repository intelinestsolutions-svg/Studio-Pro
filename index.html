FAQ

Questions, answered.

What clients usually ask before starting — including what a website costs.

How much does a website cost?
Websites including hosting and domain name start at as low as RM1,600.00. Anything bigger — more pages, SEO, 3D extras — is quoted clearly at the planning stage, so you approve a fixed price before we build. No hidden charges, no surprises at the end.
How long does a website take?
Most sites go live in 2–4 weeks from wireframe to launch, with your feedback at every step. Larger 3D builds take a little longer, and the schedule is confirmed with you before the work starts.
Do I need my content and logo ready first?
No. You only need enough to describe what you offer. We map the structure and write a content outline for every page, then work with your logo, photos and text as they arrive. Prefer us to write it? Ask us to add copywriting to your project.
Will people find it on Google?
Yes — smart on-page SEO is part of the build: titles and headings, structured data, sitemaps submitted to Google and Search Console, analytics installed, then a monthly roadmap of new pages, content and local signals that keeps rankings climbing.
Will it be fast and work on phones?
Every site is responsive by default and tuned for speed while we design — compressed images, lazy loading, clean code and caching. We test across browsers and devices before launch, so it feels as quick on a phone as it does on a desktop.
Who owns the website when it's done?
You do. Design files, source code, content and every login belong to you — nothing is locked to us. You also get a recorded training session and a written guide so your team can make everyday changes without calling a developer.
